# Blast Party

Movement is continuous and analogue, with collision-checked corner assistance.
Bombs, crates and blast paths remain on tiles. A planted bomb lets overlapping
players leave once, but blocks re-entry. Kick preserves its owner and fuse.

Start / Enter opens the pause menu. Stick or D-pad navigates; left/right changes
values, A selects, B or Start resumes. Back still returns to GameNight.

The menu and companion app share the declared settings: AI opponents (0–3,
limited by the four total places), Free-for-all / Teams, arena, team damage,
crate density, pickup chance and bomb fuse. Changes apply next round. Select
Start new round in the pause menu to apply them immediately. AI never displaces
a joined player or claims a controller. In teams, positions 1+3 face 2+4;
the rings below characters show their team. Team damage is off by default,
but your own bombs still hurt you. The last surviving team earns the round.

There is no timed victory. Rounds finish by elimination and repeat with a new
arena. Arena choices are Courtyard (open middle and side cover), Crossroads
(wide lanes), Arcades (broken parallel barriers), Pillars (classic weaving),
or Random. Mirrored layouts preserve safe exits and connected terrain.
Line/beam and diagonal pickups have been removed. Remaining upgrades are range,
capacity, speed, kick, remote detonation, eight-way and the standard cross.

Visual concepts A (Toybox), B (Garden Mayhem), and C (Space Snacks) are awaiting
selection; generated concepts are not shipped game art yet.
